# botplate for baseplate - a discord economy simulator

we welcome you to baseplate! a micronation of discordia

## What is botplate?

botplate is the finishing touch to a low effort, not thought out, poorly made discord economy simulator.

I do think you'll like it here though.

## info sheet

botplate powers baseplate: the bank, the casino (for some reason.), shops, and like.. everything!

its built on top of `poise`, using `sea-orm` for the database

### Commands

- `/help` - get a list of all available commands, and a manual on how to use them
- `/ping` - check the bot's latency
- `/info` - get some info about botplate
- `/balance` - check your tuxbux balance
- `/daily` - claim your daily tuxbux (24h cooldown)
- `/rank` - check your level and XP
- `/gamble` - gamble tuxbux with a 50/50 chance of winning or losing, doubling on a win
- `/inventory` - check your inventory
- `/shop create` - create a new shop
- `/shop manage` - manage your shop (rename, etc.)
- `/shop delete` - delete your shop 
- `/shop list` - view items listed in the current shop
- `/buy` - buy an item from a shop
- `/items sell` - list an item for sale in your shop
- `/items manage` - add/reduce stock of an item from your shop
- `/items delete` - delete an item from your shop
